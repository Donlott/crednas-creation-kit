# Progression & Difficulty

Select **Progression & Difficulty**, then choose its source and section. This module exposes supported additional progression, difference/adjustment, condition and nested skill values beyond the basic learning-list and GLID views.

Edit the exact source and row you intend to change. Primary, auxiliary and difficulty-specific values remain separate; the editor does not assume that one source overrides another. Grouped conditions and nested entries keep their structure.

Reference pickers and connected links help inspect supported classes, characters, items and skills. Unknown category/adjustment codes remain raw values, and the resulting growth or difficulty formula is not simulated.

Use [Learning tables](Learning.md) for learn-list expansion and [GLID progression](GLID.md) for stored automatic level cells. A change in one progression module does not automatically rewrite the others or establish a complete progression path.
